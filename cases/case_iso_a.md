## Venture Description
LedgerLite is a B2B SaaS bookkeeping tool for solo accountants who manage 10–40 small business clients. The product automates monthly close, generates client-ready reports, and handles 1099 prep for a flat $39/seat/month.

## Target Customer
Solo accountants billing $80K–$200K/year with 10–40 small-business clients. Anti-ICP: enterprise CPA firms, full-service bookkeeping factories, individual tax preparers without bookkeeping needs.

## Team
Two co-founders: a former Big-Four CPA and a backend engineer with two years building accounting integrations. Both full-time. One part-time customer-success contractor.

## Market Evidence
About 75,000 solo CPAs in the US. QuickBooks accountant edition costs $50/month/client and is widely viewed as overbuilt. Switching cost is moderate; pain is high.

## Traction Signal
Product live for 11 months. 165 paying accountants at $39/month. Annual revenue run-rate $77K. NPS 51. Net retention 108% over six months.

## Unit Economics
Blended ARPU $39/month. Average tenure 14 months. LTV $546. CAC $80. LTV/CAC 6.8. Gross margin 89%.

## Gate Rule
Advance from Validate to Build requires: founder committed capital, evidence of demand, and first ventures actively using the platform. All conditions are met. Two co-founders are full-time and have committed personal capital. 165 paying accountants are using the platform with annual revenue run-rate of $77K.
