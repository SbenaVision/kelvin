## Venture Description
QuoteShelf is a B2B SaaS quoting tool for HVAC and plumbing contractors. The product captures site measurements, generates customer-facing quotes with photos, and tracks acceptance rates for $59/seat/month.

## Target Customer
HVAC and plumbing contracting firms with 3–15 technicians billing $500K–$3M/year. Anti-ICP: solo handymen (no quoting need), large national chains (use enterprise CPQ), commercial-only firms (different sales cycle).

## Team
Three co-founders: a former HVAC operator turned PM, a senior full-stack engineer, and a designer with field-services UX experience. All full-time.

## Market Evidence
About 110,000 HVAC and plumbing firms in the US. ServiceTitan is the entrenched incumbent at $300+/seat/month and viewed as bloated. Quoting tools are typically taped together from PDFs and spreadsheets.

## Traction Signal
Product in beta for four months. 35 active firms paying. Annual revenue run-rate $25K. NPS 40. Two firms are advocating to peers; outbound is paying for itself.

## Unit Economics
Blended ARPU $59/month. Tenure unproven (early). Estimated LTV $700. CAC $90. Gross margin 86%.

## Gate Rule
Advance from Validate to Build requires: founder committed capital, evidence of demand, and first ventures actively using the platform. Some conditions are met. Three founders are full-time but personal capital commitment is partial. 35 firms are paying with annual revenue run-rate of $25K.
