## Venture Description
RootStack is a developer-tools SaaS for site reliability engineers managing fleets of 50–500 microservices. The product automates blast-radius tracing across services and produces incident-postmortem drafts inside Slack within 90 seconds.

## Target Customer
SRE leads at engineering orgs of 100–800 engineers running microservice architectures on Kubernetes. Anti-ICP: monolith shops, single-service teams, organizations with strict on-prem-only requirements.

## Team
Three co-founders: a former staff SRE at a public-traded fintech, a distributed-systems engineer who shipped two prior observability products, and a go-to-market lead with five years selling into engineering orgs. All full-time. Two senior engineers on payroll.

## Market Evidence
Roughly 12,000 mid-market engineering orgs in the US run Kubernetes microservice fleets at this scale. Datadog, PagerDuty, and FireHydrant cover adjacent slices but none is purpose-built for blast-radius tracing. Switching cost is moderate; integration cost is high but one-time.
