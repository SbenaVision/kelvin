## s01

ReadyRounds is a "computational layer for hemodynamic reasoning" specifically targeting the complex assessment of multi-valve heart disease in high-acuity environments like the Cardiac Intensive Care Unit (CICU).

## s03

Key deliverables include primary driver ranking (identifying which lesion most explains the clinical picture), a counterfactual simulator (predicting how fixing one valve will affect the severity of others), a consistency report (flagging measurements that contradict physical principles), and next-best-test guidance (identifying the specific measurement that would most reduce diagnostic uncertainty).

## s04

Clinicians working in high-acuity cardiac environments such as the Cardiac Intensive Care Unit (CICU), assessing patients with multi-valve heart disease.

## s06

In multi-valve disease, standard measurements are often coupled and unreliable — the severity of one valve depends on another — and clinicians currently lack a systematic method to decompose these interdependencies, leading to fragmented and slow decision-making.
