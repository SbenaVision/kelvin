## s01

narma is an "Enterprise Agentic Brain" designed for AI-first companies to see, prove, and deploy AI automation at scale.

## s02

The architecture has three layers.

## s03

Observe (Layer 1): lightweight desktop sensors capture work patterns including application usage, activity sequences, and time allocation.

## s04

Intelligence Layer (Layer 2): a "Brain" that detects employee intent, the business value of tasks, and "drift" where work diverges from the intended flow.

## s05

Execution Layer (Layer 3): an "Agent Factory" that automatically generates, deploys, and monitors AI agents for validated high-ROI opportunities with built-in approval gates.

## s07

Vision: a single system that observes how work happens, decides what AI should do next, and auto-builds agents continuously to ensure a "Continuous AI lift." AI-first companies seeking to see, prove, and deploy AI automation at scale.
