## Venture Description

Charlie Health is a hyperlocal mental-health marketplace matching therapists to clients by modality, insurance, and same-week availability. Patients book in under two minutes. Therapists get filled schedules without running marketing.

## Target Customer

Adults seeking therapy in urban U.S. markets with insurance they cannot reliably use on existing platforms. Secondary ICP: independent therapists underutilized by 20–40%.

## Traction Signal

80 paying subscribers at $7/month after nine months of beta. Modest but real retention — half the original cohort is still active. Growth is flat month-over-month without paid acquisition.

## Traction Signal

Nine paid design partners at $1.5k/month MRR. Two pilots converted to annual in the first 30 days. Organic referrals from the design-partner cohort driving roughly one qualified inbound per week.

## Market Evidence

Headspace and BetterHelp dominate self-pay but avoid insurance. The gap is real: 62% of U.S. adults who "tried therapy" reported insurance friction as the blocker. Provider supply is abundant and underutilized.

## Traction Signal

2,100 booked sessions in the last quarter. 48% of patients book a second session within 14 days. Strong word-of-mouth coefficient — 31% of new patients arrive via direct referral from existing ones.
