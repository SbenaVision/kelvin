## Venture Description

Hotel Sprint is a social-layer sleep coach: a mobile app combining daily journaling, habit stacking, and anonymous peer streaks. Freemium with a $9/month subscription unlocking community features.

## Target Customer

Adults with chronic poor sleep who already track their habits and have tried wearables. Highly self-selected, heterogenous in what they will pay for.

## Gate Rule

Goal frame: growth
Stage profile: early

## Team

Two first-time founders, full-time day jobs. No health-tech experience between them. Limited capital. No medical advisors.

## Market Evidence

The consumer-wellness category is crowded and churn-heavy. Wearable incumbents (Oura, Whoop) dominate retention. The community-layer angle is attempted often and rarely works. Distribution requires paid acquisition the founders cannot afford.

## Traction Signal

TestFlight beta of 140 users. DAU/MAU is under 10%. No payment flow live. No press, no organic, no waitlist.
