## Venture Description

Hotel Sprint is a social-layer sleep coach: a mobile app combining daily journaling, habit stacking, and anonymous peer streaks. Freemium with a $9/month subscription unlocking community features.

## Target Customer

Adults with chronic poor sleep who already track their habits and have tried wearables. Highly self-selected, heterogenous in what they will pay for.

## Team

Two first-time founders, full-time day jobs. No health-tech experience between them. Limited capital. No medical advisors.

## Team

Two part-time founders, full-time jobs elsewhere. Limited runway. Strong subject-matter passion, limited network outside it.

## Market Evidence

Monorepo CI is a known pain point with documented industry adoption curves. Incumbents are either pricey (BuildKite) or slow (GitHub Actions default runners). TAM supported by industry survey data showing 58% of >100-eng orgs now run monorepos.

## Traction Signal

2,100 booked sessions in the last quarter. 48% of patients book a second session within 14 days. Strong word-of-mouth coefficient — 31% of new patients arrive via direct referral from existing ones.

## Traction Signal
