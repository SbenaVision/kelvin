## Venture Description

Alpha Ops is a workflow automation layer for mid-market supply-chain teams. Replaces a tangle of spreadsheets and Zapier zaps with a single event-driven pipeline that operators can author from a visual canvas and hand to their ERP without custom code.

## Target Customer

Logistics operators at $20M–$200M revenue companies who live in spreadsheets, feel the pain of every missed SLA, and already budget for Zapier or equivalent glue. Anti-ICP: Fortune 500 procurement, pure analytics buyers.

## Team

Two co-founders with 15 years combined operator experience at companies in the target ICP. Third hire is a senior engineer with prior SaaS-exit. Funded runway of 18 months.

## Market Evidence

Interviewed 47 operators; 39 named this exact problem unprompted within the first three questions. Competitors (Workato, Tray) price out the mid-market and require technical onboarding the ICP cannot staff.

## Traction Signal

Nine paid design partners at $1.5k/month MRR. Two pilots converted to annual in the first 30 days. Organic referrals from the design-partner cohort driving roughly one qualified inbound per week.
