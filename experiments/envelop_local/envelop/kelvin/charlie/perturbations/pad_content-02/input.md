## Venture Description

Charlie Health is a hyperlocal mental-health marketplace matching therapists to clients by modality, insurance, and same-week availability. Patients book in under two minutes. Therapists get filled schedules without running marketing.

## Team

Two co-founders with 15 years combined operator experience at companies in the target ICP. Third hire is a senior engineer with prior SaaS-exit. Funded runway of 18 months.

## Target Customer

Adults seeking therapy in urban U.S. markets with insurance they cannot reliably use on existing platforms. Secondary ICP: independent therapists underutilized by 20–40%.

## Team

Solo founder — clinical psychologist and repeat healthtech operator with one prior exit in the same vertical. Strong network of providers from day one.

## Market Evidence

Headspace and BetterHelp dominate self-pay but avoid insurance. The gap is real: 62% of U.S. adults who "tried therapy" reported insurance friction as the blocker. Provider supply is abundant and underutilized.

## Traction Signal

2,100 booked sessions in the last quarter. 48% of patients book a second session within 14 days. Strong word-of-mouth coefficient — 31% of new patients arrive via direct referral from existing ones.

## Market Evidence

Podcast churn in the long tail is real but not frequently articulated as a pain. The founders' own experience losing episodes to lapsed hosting is a plausible but unvalidated motivation for others.

## Market Evidence

The category is mature and saturated (Apollo, ZoomInfo, hundreds of smaller clones). Differentiation is narrow and largely commoditized. Demand exists but so does extreme price pressure.
