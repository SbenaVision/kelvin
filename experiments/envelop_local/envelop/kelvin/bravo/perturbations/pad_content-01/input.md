## Venture Description

Bravo Forge is a developer-tools company shipping a managed CI runner for monorepos. Charges per cached-task-minute, with a free tier that scales naturally into paid as teams grow beyond a handful of services.

## Market Evidence

Interviewed 47 operators; 39 named this exact problem unprompted within the first three questions. Competitors (Workato, Tray) price out the mid-market and require technical onboarding the ICP cannot staff.

## Target Customer

Platform engineers at 50–500-person engineering orgs running Nx, Turborepo, or Bazel. Teams who feel every minute of CI bleed and already pay for GitHub Actions concurrency they cannot fully utilize.

## Venture Description

Charlie Health is a hyperlocal mental-health marketplace matching therapists to clients by modality, insurance, and same-week availability. Patients book in under two minutes. Therapists get filled schedules without running marketing.

## Market Evidence

Monorepo CI is a known pain point with documented industry adoption curves. Incumbents are either pricey (BuildKite) or slow (GitHub Actions default runners). TAM supported by industry survey data showing 58% of >100-eng orgs now run monorepos.

## Traction Signal

340 active teams on the free tier after six weeks of launch. 62 have upgraded to paid. Net dollar retention above 100% on the first cohort. Active community around the open-core CLI.
