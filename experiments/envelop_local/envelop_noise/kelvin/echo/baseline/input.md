## Venture Description

Echo Signal is a lightweight observability layer for small engineering teams. Sits between Sentry and Datadog in scope, priced for teams who find both too expensive or too heavyweight. Self-serve onboarding, usage-based pricing.

## Target Customer

Engineering teams of 5–25 at seed-stage SaaS companies. Teams that have outgrown console.log and cannot justify enterprise observability spend yet.

## Team

Founder-engineer plus one part-time co-founder on sales. Shipping since early in the year. Small but dedicated advisor bench.

## Market Evidence

Observability tooling is crowded but the middle-market pricing gap is well-known. Several adjacent tools (Highlight, Baselime) are chasing the same wedge with mixed traction. Signal-to-noise on the category is mid.

## Traction Signal

140 active teams across free and paid tiers. Conversion from free to $99/month is ~14%. Expansion revenue is flat; most paid teams stay on entry-level plan.
