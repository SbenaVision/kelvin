## Venture Description

Echo Signal is a lightweight observability layer for small engineering teams. Sits between Sentry and Datadog in scope, priced for teams who find both too expensive or too heavyweight. Self-serve onboarding, usage-based pricing.

## Target Customer

Engineering teams of 5–25 at seed-stage SaaS companies. Teams that have outgrown console.log and cannot justify enterprise observability spend yet.
