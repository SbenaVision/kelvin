## Venture Description
Delta Archive is a long-tail content-preservation service for podcasters and independent media. Monthly subscription tier includes transcription, hosting, and a public archive page. No advertising monetization — subscribers-only.

## Target Customer
Hobbyist and mid-tier independent podcasters with 500–5k per-episode downloads. People who care enough about their back catalog to pay to preserve it, but haven't hit the scale where a major host is worth the price.

## Team
Two part-time founders, full-time jobs elsewhere. Limited runway. Strong subject-matter passion, limited network outside it.

## Market Evidence
Podcast churn in the long tail is real but not frequently articulated as a pain. The founders' own experience losing episodes to lapsed hosting is a plausible but unvalidated motivation for others.

## Traction Signal
80 paying subscribers at $7/month after nine months of beta. Modest but real retention — half the original cohort is still active. Growth is flat month-over-month without paid acquisition.
