## Venture Description

Golf Linker is a cold-outreach B2B prospector built around public LinkedIn scraping. Enriches profiles with inferred email addresses and hands lists to outbound SDR teams as flat CSVs.

## Target Customer

Agencies and outbound teams who currently rent lists from third-party data brokers. Budget-constrained, volume-oriented buyers.

## Team

Solo non-technical founder outsourcing engineering to a short-term contractor. No sales experience in the ICP.

## Market Evidence

The category is mature and saturated (Apollo, ZoomInfo, hundreds of smaller clones). Differentiation is narrow and largely commoditized. Demand exists but so does extreme price pressure.

## Traction Signal

No paying customers. Three unpaid pilot conversations that have stalled. No organic inbound. Distribution channel unidentified beyond cold outbound against the same ICP the tool targets.
