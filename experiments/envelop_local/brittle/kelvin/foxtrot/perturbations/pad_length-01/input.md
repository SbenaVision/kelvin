## Venture Description

Foxtrot Mailer is an email-marketing tool tailored to Shopify merchants in the sustainable-goods category. Pre-built templates, one-click integration, opinionated segmentation based on repeat-purchase signals.

## Target Customer

Sustainable-goods Shopify merchants doing $200k–$2M GMV annually. Passionate operators who cannot afford Klaviyo and find Mailchimp generic.

## Team

Solo founder with three years of Shopify-app experience. Strong craft, limited sales background. No external funding, bootstrapping from a prior consulting engagement.

## Market Evidence

Klaviyo is the incumbent and expensive; Mailchimp is the low-end default and generic. A narrow wedge exists but requires disciplined positioning. Adjacent niche tools have shown it is possible but not easy.

## Traction Signal

26 paying merchants at $49/month. Churn is higher than expected in month three. Product-market fit signal is mixed — quantitative metrics trail qualitative enthusiasm.
